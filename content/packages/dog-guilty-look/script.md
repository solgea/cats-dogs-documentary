## Central question

Does a dog's guilty look mean the dog feels guilty?

## Verified core fact

A dog's guilty look is influenced by owner cues and scolding, not by whether the dog actually disobeyed.

## Takeaway

Read the situation and the dog's behavior instead of treating the look as a confession.

# Visual plan - The Guilty Look Isn't Guilt

**Package:** `dog-guilty-look`  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 45 seconds  
**Disclosure:** Real-footage montage using third-party clips for illustration; not documentary footage or scientific evidence.

## Global direction

- **Style:** Observational real-footage montage built from third-party pet clips, natural light, calm pacing, and species-correct behavior.
- **Anatomy:** Every animal must be species-correct and anatomically coherent. Multiple individuals may appear across clips; reject malformed, uncanny, or merged animals.
- **Welfare:** The dog is calm or mildly uncertain, never frightened, injured, punished, or physically corrected. No shouting, leash jerks, or staged distress. Reject any clip with staged distress, restraint, injury, aggression, or a visible watermark.
- **Continuity:** Keep the question, tone, and caption position consistent across clips. Do not imply that different animals are the same individual. Avoid repeated or watermarked clips.
- **Negative prompt:** No extra limbs, no extra ears, no extra tails, no distorted eyes, no malformed or merged animals, no text artifacts, no logos, no watermarks, no social handles, no distressed animals, no staged aggression, no implied documentary evidence.

## Scene-by-scene direction

### 1. s1 (0:00-0:02)

- **Shot:** eye-level medium close-up
- **Subject:** one brown mixed-breed dog with floppy ears and a white chest patch
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, dog's face in the upper middle third, caption-safe lower third
- **Action:** A medium-sized brown mixed-breed dog sits on a soft rug in a calm living room, looking up toward an off-screen owner with ears slightly back and tail tucked low. Soft window light, shallow depth of field, restrained documentary mood.
- **Camera motion:** slow push-in, dog holds the look and blinks once
- **Continuity:** same dog, rug, and warm living room as every later scene; no collar changes
- **Avoid:** human face, raised hand, crying or cowering, text overlays

### 2. s2 (0:02-0:10)

- **Shot:** low-angle wide shot
- **Subject:** the same brown dog and a human hand, no visible face
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, bowl in lower third, dog in middle third, clear floor space
- **Action:** A simple kitchen scene: a metal food bowl sits on a tiled floor while the same brown dog watches from a respectful distance. An owner's hand enters frame and places the bowl, then withdraws. The dog stays calm and curious.
- **Camera motion:** gentle lateral slide from bowl to dog
- **Continuity:** same dog and coat markings; kitchen adjacent to the living room; bowl is brushed steel
- **Avoid:** food aggression, snarling, human face, messy food

### 3. s3 (0:10-0:18)

- **Shot:** over-the-shoulder medium shot from behind the owner
- **Subject:** the same brown dog; owner visible only as a shoulder and open hands

- **Camera motion:** subtle handheld drift, no sudden movement
- **Continuity:** same living room, rug, and dog; owner wears a plain oatmeal sweater

- **Continuity:** same rug, sweater, and dog; no new people or animals
- **Avoid:** sad music cue, human face, leash tugging, text overlays

## Welfare and authenticity review

- No scene asks for fear, pain, injury, aggression, or staged distress.
- Real footage is illustrative B-roll, not evidence; never present a clip as an archival record of the claim.
- Every clip must show species-correct, anatomically coherent animals; reject malformed or uncanny animals.
- Reject watermarks, logos, social handles, burned-in captions, extra limbs, or merged faces.
- Credit every third-party clip source in the YouTube description.
- Keep captions in the lower third and outside the main subject's face.

# Visual plan - The Cry Hidden in a Cat's Purr

**Package:** `purr-cry`  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 45 seconds  
**Disclosure:** Real-footage montage using third-party clips for illustration; not documentary footage or scientific evidence.

## Global direction

- **Style:** Observational real-footage montage built from third-party pet clips, natural light, calm pacing, and species-correct behavior.
- **Anatomy:** Every animal must be species-correct and anatomically coherent. Multiple individuals may appear across clips; reject malformed, uncanny, or merged animals.
- **Welfare:** The cat is calm, fed a small plain portion, and never shown starving, begging in distress, or eating unsafe food. No restraint or forced interaction. Reject any clip with staged distress, restraint, injury, aggression, or a visible watermark.
- **Continuity:** Keep the question, tone, and caption position consistent across clips. Do not imply that different animals are the same individual. Avoid repeated or watermarked clips.
- **Negative prompt:** No extra limbs, no extra ears, no extra tails, no distorted eyes, no malformed or merged animals, no text artifacts, no logos, no watermarks, no social handles, no distressed animals, no staged aggression, no implied documentary evidence.

## Scene-by-scene direction

### 1. s1 (0:00-0:02)

- **Shot:** low-angle close-up
- **Subject:** one gray cat with a white chest and pale yellow eyes
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, cat's face in the upper middle third, bowl at the bottom edge
- **Action:** A gray cat sits on a kitchen floor beside a ceramic food bowl, looking up toward an off-screen person. Its mouth is closed and its throat is relaxed, as if a low purr is running. Soft morning light and a quiet, uncluttered kitchen.
- **Camera motion:** slow push-in; the cat blinks once and holds a calm gaze
- **Continuity:** same gray cat, kitchen, and ceramic bowl in every scene
- **Avoid:** human face, food mess, starving appearance, text overlays

### 2. s2 (0:02-0:10)

- **Shot:** abstract macro animation
- **Subject:** two layered ribbons of light, one low and broad, one higher and thinner
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, ribbons centered with generous negative space above
- **Action:** A warm abstract visualization of a purr: a broad, slow undulating ribbon of amber light fills the lower half of the frame, while a thinner, brighter thread rises through it. The shapes are smooth and non-literal, with no labels or readable marks.
- **Camera motion:** slow lateral drift; the bright thread gradually becomes more distinct
- **Continuity:** same amber palette and fine grain as the kitchen scenes; no sudden style shift
- **Avoid:** readable characters, musical notes, flashing lights, text overlays

### 3. s3 (0:10-0:18)

- **Shot:** medium shot, slightly low angle
- **Subject:** the same gray cat and an owner's hand, no visible face
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, cat and bowl in the lower middle third, clean space above
- **Action:** The same gray cat stands beside the ceramic bowl in the kitchen. An owner's hand lowers a small portion of plain food into the bowl and withdraws. The cat watches the hand, then looks toward the bowl without rushing or vocalizing visibly.
- **Camera motion:** gentle tilt down from hand to bowl
- **Continuity:** same kitchen, bowl, and cat; the owner wears a plain gray sleeve
- **Avoid:** food aggression, human face, overflowing bowl, text overlays

### 4. s4 (0:18-0:26)

- **Shot:** abstract close-up animation
- **Subject:** the bright upper thread and the broad lower ribbon of light
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, crest on the upper-third line, lower ribbon across the bottom third
- **Action:** A closer abstract view of the same layered sound image. The bright thread now has a clear, narrow crest near the upper third of the frame, while the broad amber ribbon continues below. Tiny particles of light drift along both paths, and no labels appear.
- **Camera motion:** slow push-in toward the crest, then a gentle hold
- **Continuity:** same amber palette, grain, and ribbon shapes as the previous abstract scene
- **Avoid:** readable characters, scientific labels, flashing lights, text overlays

### 5. s5 (0:26-0:35)

- **Shot:** medium close-up
- **Subject:** the same gray cat, the ceramic bowl, and an owner's resting hand
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, cat in the middle third, hand and bowl in the lower third
- **Action:** The same gray cat sits beside the bowl while an owner's hand rests on the counter nearby. The cat looks toward the hand, half-closes its eyes, and stays calm. A soft reflection of the kitchen window moves across the ceramic bowl.
- **Camera motion:** very slow lateral slide from the cat's face to the bowl
- **Continuity:** same cat, bowl, counter, and gray sleeve; no other animals
- **Avoid:** human face, grabbing the bowl, food mess, text overlays

### 6. s6 (0:35-0:45)

- **Shot:** medium shot, then slow pull-back
- **Subject:** the same gray cat resting beside the ceramic bowl
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, cat in the lower middle third, window light above
- **Action:** The same gray cat rests on a windowsill beside the bowl, which holds a little plain food. The cat's eyes are half closed and its breathing is slow. The camera pulls back gently to show the quiet kitchen, then fades.
- **Camera motion:** slow pull-back; the cat remains still and calm
- **Continuity:** same windowsill, bowl, and gray cat; no new people or animals
- **Avoid:** empty bowl close-up, crying cat, human face, text overlays

## Welfare and authenticity review

- No scene asks for fear, pain, injury, aggression, or staged distress.
- Real footage is illustrative B-roll, not evidence; never present a clip as an archival record of the claim.
- Every clip must show species-correct, anatomically coherent animals; reject malformed or uncanny animals.
- Reject watermarks, logos, social handles, burned-in captions, extra limbs, or merged faces.
- Credit every third-party clip source in the YouTube description.
- Keep captions in the lower third and outside the main subject's face.

## Central question

Why does a hungry cat's purr sound so urgent?

## Verified core fact

In a 2009 study, solicitation purrs contained a pronounced high-frequency component around 380 Hz, and listeners rated them more urgent and less pleasant than ordinary purrs.

## Takeaway

The cry is a real acoustic feature, but it does not prove that a cat is calculating or suffering.

## Central question

Why do dogs have that expressive inner-eyebrow look?

## Verified core fact

Dogs have a facial muscle that raises the inner eyebrow, and the dogs studied produced that movement more often and intensely than wolves.

## Takeaway

Puppy-dog eyes are built into dog anatomy. The idea that humans selected for them is a strong hypothesis, not a proven cause.

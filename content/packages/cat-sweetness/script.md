## Central question

Why can't cats taste sweetness?

## Verified core fact

Domestic cats carry an unexpressed Tas1r2 pseudogene, so the two-part sweet-taste receptor cannot form.

## Takeaway

A cat's indifference to sweets is receptor biology, not pickiness or a preference for expensive food.

## Central question

Do cats understand a human slow blink?

## Verified core fact

In two experiments, cats showed more eye narrowing and half-blinks when a human slow-blinked at them, and they were more likely to approach an unfamiliar experimenter after a slow blink.

## Takeaway

A slow blink may be a low-pressure greeting, but it is not a guaranteed sign of trust; read the cat's whole response.

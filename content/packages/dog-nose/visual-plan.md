# Visual plan - Why a Dog's Nose Works in Stereo

**Package:** `dog-nose`  
**Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, 45 seconds  
**Disclosure:** Real-footage montage using third-party clips for illustration; not documentary footage or scientific evidence.

## Global direction

- **Style:** Observational real-footage montage built from third-party pet clips, natural light, calm pacing, and species-correct behavior.
- **Anatomy:** Every animal must be species-correct and anatomically coherent. Multiple individuals may appear across clips; reject malformed, uncanny, or merged animals.
- **Welfare:** The dog is calm, curious, and unrestrained in a quiet garden. No leash jerking, force, injury, or distress. The model sequence contains no dissection or animal tissue. Reject any clip with staged distress, restraint, injury, aggression, or a visible watermark.
- **Continuity:** Keep the question, tone, and caption position consistent across clips. Do not imply that different animals are the same individual. Avoid repeated or watermarked clips.
- **Negative prompt:** No extra limbs, no extra ears, no extra tails, no distorted eyes, no malformed or merged animals, no text artifacts, no logos, no watermarks, no social handles, no distressed animals, no staged aggression, no implied documentary evidence.

## Scene-by-scene direction

### 1. s1 (0:00-0:02)

- **Shot:** low-angle close-up
- **Subject:** one black Labrador mix with a graying muzzle and a glossy black nose
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, nose in the lower middle third, eye on the upper-third line
- **Action:** A black Labrador mix with a graying muzzle lowers its nose to a fallen leaf on a garden path. The camera frames the snout and one relaxed eye, with soft green foliage blurred behind. The dog sniffs calmly and lifts its head slightly.
- **Camera motion:** slow push-in as the dog sniffs the leaf
- **Continuity:** same black dog, red collar, and garden path in every scene
- **Avoid:** human face, leash tugging, wet or injured nose, text overlays

### 2. s2 (0:02-0:10)

- **Shot:** macro three-quarter view of the snout
- **Subject:** the same black dog's nose and muzzle, with two abstract airflow ribbons
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, nostrils centered, ribbons flowing into the upper and lower thirds
- **Action:** The same black dog holds its nose close to the leaf while two faint, translucent ribbons of air curl toward the nostrils. One ribbon rises along the upper snout, the other runs lower. The dog stays still and curious, and the garden light is soft.
- **Camera motion:** very slow lateral drift; the ribbons divide and settle
- **Continuity:** same glossy nose, graying muzzle, and red collar edge
- **Avoid:** smoke, fire, human hands, text overlays

### 3. s3 (0:10-0:18)

- **Shot:** studio product-style medium shot
- **Subject:** one translucent canine head model with a detailed snout
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, model centered with generous dark negative space
- **Action:** A translucent, glass-like model of a dog's head rotates slowly above a dark studio surface. The snout is anatomically correct, and a soft amber light suggests the internal nasal airway without opening the body. No labels or readable marks appear.
- **Camera motion:** slow orbital rotation, then a gentle hold
- **Continuity:** same amber palette and dark background throughout the model sequence
- **Avoid:** dissection, blood, medical labels, text overlays

### 4. s4 (0:18-0:26)

- **Shot:** abstract macro animation
- **Subject:** two separate airflow ribbons, one high and narrow, one low and broad
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, both ribbons centered with clear separation
- **Action:** An abstract view of two smooth ribbons of light moving through a dark amber space. The upper ribbon curves toward the rear and slows into a fine mesh; the lower ribbon runs straighter and wider toward the throat. The shapes are non-literal and unlabeled.
- **Camera motion:** slow forward drift; the upper ribbon spreads into a delicate mesh
- **Continuity:** same amber palette, grain, and dark background as the model scene
- **Avoid:** readable characters, medical diagram, flashing lights, text overlays

### 5. s5 (0:26-0:35)

- **Shot:** front macro close-up
- **Subject:** the same black dog's two nostrils and muzzle, with two separate air ribbons
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, nose centered, one ribbon entering from each side
- **Action:** A front macro view of the same black dog's nose. Two separate pale ribbons curl in from different directions, one toward each nostril, with a clear gap between them. The muzzle is relaxed and the whiskers are still.
- **Camera motion:** static camera; the ribbons pulse gently inward
- **Continuity:** same glossy nose, graying muzzle, and red collar edge
- **Avoid:** merged nostrils, smoke, human hands, text overlays

### 6. s6 (0:35-0:45)

- **Shot:** medium shot, then slow pull-back
- **Subject:** the same black dog and an owner's open hand
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, dog in the lower middle third, open garden above
- **Action:** The same black dog stands on the garden path beside an owner's open hand, nose lifted toward a light breeze. The dog's posture is relaxed and its tail is loose. The camera pulls back slowly to reveal the green garden, then fades.
- **Camera motion:** slow pull-back; the dog lifts its nose and holds
- **Continuity:** same dog, red collar, garden path, and soft daylight; no other animals
- **Avoid:** human face, leash tugging, hybrid animals, text overlays

## Welfare and authenticity review

- No scene asks for fear, pain, injury, aggression, or staged distress.
- Real footage is illustrative B-roll, not evidence; never present a clip as an archival record of the claim.
- Every clip must show species-correct, anatomically coherent animals; reject malformed or uncanny animals.
- Reject watermarks, logos, social handles, burned-in captions, extra limbs, or merged faces.
- Credit every third-party clip source in the YouTube description.
- Keep captions in the lower third and outside the main subject's face.

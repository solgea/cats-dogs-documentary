## Central question

How does a dog's nose separate smelling from breathing?

## Verified core fact

A computer model built from MRI scans showed that a dog's nasal airflow splits into separate olfactory and respiratory paths, and each nostril samples a spatially separate patch of air.

## Takeaway

A dog's nose is a routing system as well as a sensor; its shape helps scent reach the olfactory region and may support comparing samples from two nostrils.
